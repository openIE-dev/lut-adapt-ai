Downward LUT6 Compilation in a Mixture of Limits Cascade

David Charlot, Open Interface Engineering

This paper studies how a Mixture of Limits cascade compiles certified neural wakes downward into Beneš-routed LUT6 memory pages, how Expert-AirMoL cuts NVMe SSD weight reads on late-2026 hybrid and sparse-MoE language models up to 34.66B parameters by up to 12.30× at 100.0% exact token parity, and how verifier-gated synthesis beats standalone frontier models with 0.00% broken commits admitted.

In plain words

  1. Most inputs a deployed system sees repeat patterns it has already solved, so running a full neural model on every step wastes compute and memory bandwidth.
  2. We put a reconfigurable 6-input truth-table (LUT6) lookup layer in front of streaming state-space and foundation models, and compile answers downward into lookup memory pages only after a strict check certifies them.
  3. When late-2026 hybrid and sparse-MoE language models up to 34.66B parameters stream 4-bit weights from NVMe (Expert-AirMoL), router-sparse expert reads plus zero-FLOP LUT6 burst drafting cut disk traffic by up to 12.30× in 2.53 GB RAM (3.83% of FP16) with zero change to output tokens; gating code commits via a deterministic verifier C(z) beats standalone 35B accuracy with 0.00% broken commits admitted and accelerates crystallized Wave-3 tasks by 35,590×.

What each measurement class is

  • Measured in software (measured_j = None): Exact Darwin F_NOCACHE os.pread NVMe byte counts, peak active RAM (250.0 MB on Oct 2026 LiquidAI/LFM3-1.7B; 1,408.3 MB on LiquidAI/LFM2.5-8B-A1B; 2,530.7 MB = 3.83% FP16 on Qwen/Qwen3.6-35B-A3B), wall-clock throughput, whole-machine macOS PMU cumulative energy deltas, and 100.0% bit/token parity on Apple M5 Max.
  • Simulated RTL (board_metered = false): Yosys 0.71+ (synth_xilinx -family xcup targeting Kria KV260 xck26) netlist synthesis (41,186 CLB LUTs, 81 RAMB36E2 blocks, 0 DSP48 slices) and Verilator 5.054 cycle simulation (CPI = 1.0). No physical KV260 bitstream or rail power was metered in this repository; the 11,428 pJ KV260 INA260 SOM reading belongs solely to Metered Commit Gates [same house].
  • Analytical energy estimates (est_j): All J/VC and mJ/byte figures come from operation counts and TDP-time formulas for within-model comparison, never silicon watts.

What is not claimed

Not claimed: physical KV260 board power for clai_benes_hotswap_core.sv; Vivado post-place-and-route timing closure at 250 MHz; silicon energy leadership over commercial ASICs; promotion of Conformal-bounded or ModelGenerated outputs to Deterministic; or capability gains beyond the greedy target checkpoint.
